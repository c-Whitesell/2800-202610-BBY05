# ShadyTrails

## Description

ShadyTrails helps people explore Vancouver’s outdoor spaces by recommending trails and parks based on weather conditions and sunlight throughout the day. Users can choose shaded or sunny routes depending on their preferences, making outdoor recreation safer and more enjoyable during hot weather. This encourages more people to spend time in nature while increasing awareness of the importance of protecting local green spaces.
